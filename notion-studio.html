OVERVIEW

Notion Studio is a feature designed to help users manage their hyperfixations—those intense, focused interests that drive deep engagement. This project explores how product design can support users in organizing and tracking their passions.

PROCESS

The design process involved user research into hyperfixation behaviors, creating intuitive interfaces for tracking interests, and designing workflows that seamlessly integrate into Notion's existing ecosystem.

RESULT

The final feature provides users with a dedicated space to explore, document, and organize their hyperfixations, making it easier to dive deep into topics they're passionate about.
