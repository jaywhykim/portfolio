MUSEO STEFANO BARDINI

Identity redesign of the Stefano Bardini Museum in Florence, Italy

FEBRUARY-APRIL 2025

BRAND DESIGN ART DIRECTION
BARD THE DEFINITIVE FLORENTINE COLLECTION.

OVERVIEW

The Museo Stefano Bardini identity redesign reimagines the visual language of one of Florence's most important museums. This project involved creating a comprehensive brand system that honors the museum's rich history while establishing a contemporary presence.

PROCESS

The design process began with extensive research into the museum's collection and history, followed by exploration of typographic and visual systems that could bridge classical and modern aesthetics. The final identity reflects the museum's role as a definitive collection of Florentine art and culture.

RESULT

The new identity system provides a cohesive visual language across all touchpoints, from print materials to digital presence, creating a memorable and distinctive brand that celebrates the museum's unique position in Florence's cultural landscape.
