Chef Yori

ROLE

Product Designer

DURATION

10 weeks, Autumn 2025

TOOL

Figma, HTML, CSS, JS, P5.JS, Adobe Illustrator

OVERVIEW

Chef Yori is a unified, delightful platform that makes discovering, saving, and organizing recipes simple in one place.

Problem

People want delicious, chef-quality meals at home but don't have the time, skills, or energy to cook them.

RESULT

The final website provides an immersive experience that captures the essence of late-night exploration, with carefully crafted interactions that guide users through the digital space.
