Chef Yori

ROLE

App Designer

DURATION

12 weeks

TOOL

Figma, React

Problem

People want delicious, chef-quality meals at home but don't have the time, skills, or energy to cook them.

Blue Ocean Strategy

I analyzed the competitive landscape to identify which features are most underrated and often overlooked by other products.

Prioritization

I used a matrix to determine the most high value and low effort features to design and develop first.

RESULT

Chef Yori is a unified, delightful platform that makes discovering, saving, and organizing recipes simple in one place.
