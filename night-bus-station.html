App Design MICROINTERACTIONS

OVERVIEW

Night Bus Station is a website designed for people who stay up late. This project explores the intersection of web design, microinteractions, and agentic-AI prototyping to create an engaging digital experience.

PROCESS

The design process involved extensive research into late-night user behaviors, creating microinteractions that respond to user engagement, and leveraging AI prototyping tools to rapidly iterate on design concepts.

RESULT

The final website provides an immersive experience that captures the essence of late-night exploration, with carefully crafted interactions that guide users through the digital space.
